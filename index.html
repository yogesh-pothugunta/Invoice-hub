Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. There are no paid plans, no ads and no watermark on your invoices.

Does it handle GST?

Yes. It splits CGST and SGST, or IGST, based on the place of supply, supports HSN/SAC codes, and gives you a monthly GST summary you can export.

How do my clients pay?

Add your UPI ID and every invoice carries a QR code, plus a Pay with UPI button on the invoice link.

Can my accountant or team use it?

Yes. Invite people as admin, accountant or viewer, and each role only sees and does what it should.

Can I send quotes and track payments?

Yes. Send quotes, convert them into invoices, and record full or partial payments.
